Clickup is an online website that teams use to collaborate on a multitude of projects. It can be connected to Github, but tasks must be manually linked to Clickup (they will not automatically show up).

SETUP:

1. Look up [Clickup.com](http://Clickup.com) on your browser  
2. Make an account on the top right (sign in with Google if that is connected to Github)  
3. In your workspace, click More, then Apps on the left sidebar  
4. Click on Github, then Settings  
5. Click Add Repo to add your repo. You can only add repos you are an admin of, so for this class, invite the TA or professor to your workspace and make them admin in Clickup so they can add the repo.

CLICKUP’S AI

On the left sidebar, there is an “Agents” section with “Brain” listed as one of the agents. This chatbot is able to pull from the entire workspace, so if you are new to a huge Clickup space, you can easily get caught up by asking Clickup Brain to summarize how everything works.

The AI is also used to create artifacts, which are little interactive apps you can use in Clickup such as specialized task trackers specifically for a particular task. It seems that the AI is the only way you can create artifacts… you cannot make them from scratch yourself.
